Click and pint, 10 bugs at 60, 180 or 300 s, 2 rounds · DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash + Gemma-4-31B on Sail

First result. When every request is a bug, delegating does not make the agent more responsive. In the arrival-rate experiment, 24 sessions, the single agent received new bugs in about 20 seconds against about 40 for the background manager, touched the files twice as fast, fixed slightly more bugs, and cost a third as much. Sending bugs faster hurt the manager more, not less. The smokes agreed: background managers beat blocking managers, but neither beat the single agent.
