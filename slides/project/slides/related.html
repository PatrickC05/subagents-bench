arXiv: 2602.11964 · 2605.27995 · 2412.07017 · 2406.12045

Recent work is starting to evaluate agents in timed, more realistic settings. Gaia2 has environments that change on their own under time limits, and even GPT-5 fails the time-sensitive tasks. AsyncTool runs several tasks at once with slow tools. AsyncLM makes tool calls non-blocking. Tau-bench puts a simulated user in the loop. What they keep fixed is the work itself: each episode has one goal, or a fixed set of tasks given at the start, and changes refine that goal. We don't restrict ourselves to that; we send new, unrelated requests while earlier work is still running. Our reading is that this is heading toward evaluating the user's experience, because the user is the one who cares how long everything takes.
