A quick map of related work. Gaia2, from Meta at ICLR this year, is the closest: environments that keep changing with time limits, and even the best model fails the time-sensitive tasks. AsyncTool runs several tasks at once with slow tools and finds delayed feedback hurts agents. AsyncLM makes function calls non-blocking with interrupts and cuts latency up to five times. Tau-bench puts a simulated user in the loop. And the alpha-UMi paper splits an agent into several smaller models. We combine these threads: a coding agent, a user whose requests overlap in time, and a large orchestrator that can hand work to small subagents.
