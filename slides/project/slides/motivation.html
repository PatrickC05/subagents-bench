Your coding agent

Busy on the first bug. Everything else waits.

If it can only do one thing at a time and you must wait for it to finish, you aren’t collaborating with it. You are offloading work.

Here is how people actually use a coding agent. You ask it to fix a bug. You get impatient and ask a question about the code. You ask how long the fix will take. Then something urgent comes up. If the agent can only do one thing at a time and you have to wait for it to finish, you aren't collaborating with it, you're just offloading work. That is the experience we want to measure and improve.
