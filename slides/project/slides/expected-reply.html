This is what we expect, not data yet. On the x-axis are the four scenarios; the bars are the three settings. With bugs only, the orchestrator alone should reply about as fast as the others. As we add estimates, questions and urgent requests, its reply time should grow, because it is busy doing the work, while the settings with background subagents should stay roughly flat. Subagents-only pays a small coordination cost everywhere.
