What do subagents buy in prior work? Anthropic's research system, a lead agent with parallel subagents, beat a single agent by about 90 percent on their research evaluation, but used about fifteen times the tokens of a chat, and they note it suits parallel work more than coding. LLMCompiler runs function calls in parallel and cuts latency up to 3.7 times. Alpha-UMi splits an agent into smaller specialists. And Cemri et al. catalogue how multi-agent systems fail, mostly in coordination. All of these ask whether subagents make the answer better or faster. We ask whether they keep the agent available.
