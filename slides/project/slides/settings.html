Our three agent settings, revealed one at a time. First, the orchestrator alone: it does all the work itself. Second, an orchestrator that can work itself or hand work to subagents running in the background, its choice. Third, an orchestrator that can only read and reply, so all the work goes to background subagents. The orchestrator and the subagents are the same model, so the settings differ only in who does the work.
